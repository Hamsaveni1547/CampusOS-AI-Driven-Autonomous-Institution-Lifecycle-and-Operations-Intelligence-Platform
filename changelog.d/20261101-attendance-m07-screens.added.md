Add the M07 Attendance screens.

- Take attendance (`/attendance`): classes from the teacher's timetable in the last 7 days still to mark; sheet with everyone present by default, one tap per student (present, absent, late, on duty, medical), counts, all present / all absent, topic notes; read-only once the edit window has passed.
- Class attendance (`/attendance/classes/{id}`, also from the class page): percentage and standing of every student.
- Attendance shortage (`/attendance-shortage`): students below the minimum per section and course, condone within the band with a reason and fee receipt.
- Attendance corrections (`/attendance-corrections`): approve or reject requests.
- My attendance (`/my-attendance`, students): percentage and standing per course, every mark, and correction requests.
